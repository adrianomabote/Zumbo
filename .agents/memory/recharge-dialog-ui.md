name: Recharge dialog UI
description: User preference for sizing the recharge panel and amount choices.
---

Keep the recharge panel wide, close to the screen edges; shrink the amount-choice buttons rather than their number labels. Keep the numbers at their original visual size.

**Why:** The user corrected the distinction between the size of the quick-amount labels and the size of their containing buttons.

**How to apply:** For future recharge-panel changes, preserve the large panel footprint and reduce button height/padding before changing label typography.
