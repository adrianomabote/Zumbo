name: Recharge dialog UI
description: User preference for sizing the recharge panel and amount choices.
---

Keep the recharge panel wide, close to the screen edges; shrink the amount-choice buttons rather than their number labels. Keep the numbers at their original visual size. Never use “Depositar” for this flow; use “Recarregar” for actions and “Recarga” for headings. Inset checkout iframes from the card edges, including the bottom, so they do not overlap its rounded corners.

**Why:** The user corrected the distinction between quick-amount labels and button size, and explicitly requested the recharge wording and inset iframe corners.

**How to apply:** For future recharge-panel changes, preserve the large panel footprint, reduce button height/padding before changing label typography, and keep the stated wording and iframe inset.
