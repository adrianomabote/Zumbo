---
name: Recharge dialog UI
description: User preference for sizing the recharge panel and amount choices.
---

Keep the recharge panel wide, close to the screen edges; shrink the amount-choice buttons rather than their number labels. Keep the numbers at their original visual size. Never use “Depositar” for this flow; use “Recarregar” for actions and “Recarga” for headings. The checkout heading should say “Recarga de saldo” without the selected amount, stay at normal size, and the checkout panel should remain centered with space below it.

**Why:** The user corrected the distinction between quick-amount labels and button size, and explicitly requested the recharge wording, checkout heading, and centered layout.

**How to apply:** For future recharge-panel changes, preserve the large panel footprint, reduce button height/padding before changing label typography, omit the selected amount from the checkout heading, and keep the centered layout with side and bottom insets.
